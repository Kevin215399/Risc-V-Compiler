RGB Led example
10/28/2026
By Kevin Hoherz

3 buttons control the red, green, and blue portion of a 4 pin led. The yellow button 
enables/disables a blinking mode.

--PINS--

2 - red button
3 - green button
4 - blue button
5 - yellow button

16 - red pin of led
17 - green pin of led
18 - blue pin of led
